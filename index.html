Add a task
Creates a card in the To do list on your Trello board.

Tasks live on your Trello board “Green Belt” and are read and written directly through Trello with your own key, which is stored only in this browser. A copy of the board stays on this device so you can see it offline, and changes made offline sync when you reconnect. Priority and organisation are the card's labels (red High, orange Medium, yellow Low; green GBU, blue RGI, purple Personal), subtasks are its “Steps” checklist, and estimates, focus time, repeats and tags are kept in the card description. Theme, filters and a running timer are remembered in this browser only.

Connect Trello

  1. Open trello.com/power-ups/admin, press New, give it any name (for example “Action Board”), choose your workspace, fill in the other required fields (the iframe connector URL can stay empty) and create it.
  2. Open its API key tab, press Generate a new API key, and copy the key into the first box.
  3. Next to the key, click the Token link, press Allow, and copy the token into the second box.

Both stay in this browser on this device and are only ever sent to Trello. You do this once per device.
